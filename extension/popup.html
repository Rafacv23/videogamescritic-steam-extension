VGC Score for Steam

On a Steam game page, the extension fetches VideoGamesCritic with the same AppID and shows the VGC Score.

  1. Open a Steam store app page, for example Resonance.
  2. Wait for the panel above the purchase block.
  3. Use the link if you want the full VGC breakdown.
Not affiliated with Valve or VideoGamesCritic. Data is requested live from videogamescritic.com.
